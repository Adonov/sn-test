Privacy Policy

Forkful · Last updated: 19 October 2026

Forkful is an app for saving recipes, planning meals and making grocery lists. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. We collect as little as possible and never sell your data or show ads.

Data stored on your device

Your recipes, photos you add, meal plan, grocery list, food diary and nutrition goals are stored on your device. You can export or delete them at any time under More.

Account (optional)

If you create an account, we store your email address, a securely hashed password, and a copy of your recipes, meal plan, grocery list and food diary so they can be synced between your devices. This data is stored with our database provider Supabase in the European Union (Ireland). You can delete your account and all of its cloud data at any time in the app under More → Account → Delete account.

Translating recipes

When you switch the app language, recipes written in another language are translated automatically. To do this, the recipe text (title, ingredients, steps, notes and tags) is sent through our server to Anthropic, an AI provider, which returns the translation. No account details are included. Anthropic processes this data on our behalf and does not use it to train its models.

Food diary and nutrition estimates

When you scan a barcode or search for a food, the barcode or search words are sent to Open Food Facts, an open food database, to look up the product. When you ask for an AI estimate – from a photo of your meal, a description or a recipe – the photo or text is sent through our server to Anthropic, which returns the estimated foods and nutrients. Photos are not stored on our server. Nutrition estimates are approximate and are not medical advice.

Apple Health and Health Connect (optional)

If you connect Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android), Forkful reads your steps, workouts with the calories burned, and your weight (for example from a smart scale). Nothing is written to your health data. Steps and workouts are kept only on your device and are used to show your activity and, if you choose, to add workout calories to your daily goal. Weight readings are added to your weight log in the app, which is synced to your account only if you use one. Health data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or stored in iCloud. You can disconnect at any time in the app, or revoke access in the Health app or Health Connect settings.

Reminders, fasting and widgets

Reminders are scheduled on your device as local notifications; no push service is used. Your fasting timer is stored with your other data. The home screen widgets show today’s numbers, which the app shares with them on your device only.

Importing recipes

When you import a recipe from a link, the app (or our server, for the website) downloads that web page to read the recipe. We do not keep a record of the links you import.

Server and logs

Our server is hosted by Render in the European Union (Frankfurt). Like any web server it briefly processes your IP address to answer requests and to prevent abuse (rate limiting). We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising tools.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your data in the app at any time. If you are in the EU, you have the rights described in the GDPR, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Forkful is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Politika zasebnosti

Forkful · Zadnja posodobitev: 19. oktober 2026

Forkful je aplikacija za shranjevanje receptov, načrtovanje obrokov in nakupovalne sezname. Ta politika pojasnjuje, katere podatke aplikacija obdeluje in zakaj. Zbiramo čim manj podatkov, jih nikoli ne prodajamo in ne prikazujemo oglasov.

Podatki na tvoji napravi

Recepti, dodane fotografije, načrt obrokov, nakupovalni seznam, prehranski dnevnik in prehranski cilji so shranjeni na tvoji napravi. Kadarkoli jih lahko izvoziš ali izbrišeš v razdelku Več.

Račun (neobvezno)

Če ustvariš račun, hranimo tvoj e-naslov, varno zgoščeno geslo ter kopijo receptov, načrta obrokov, nakupovalnega seznama in prehranskega dnevnika, da se lahko sinhronizirajo med tvojimi napravami. Ti podatki so shranjeni pri ponudniku baze Supabase v Evropski uniji (Irska). Račun in vse njegove podatke v oblaku lahko kadarkoli izbrišeš v aplikaciji pod Več → Račun → Izbriši račun.

Prevajanje receptov

Ko zamenjaš jezik aplikacije, se recepti v drugem jeziku samodejno prevedejo. Za to se besedilo recepta (naslov, sestavine, koraki, opombe in oznake) prek našega strežnika pošlje ponudniku umetne inteligence Anthropic, ki vrne prevod. Podatki o računu niso vključeni. Anthropic te podatke obdeluje v našem imenu in jih ne uporablja za učenje svojih modelov.

Prehranski dnevnik in ocene hranil

Ko skeniraš črtno kodo ali iščeš živilo, se črtna koda oziroma iskalni niz pošlje v odprto bazo Open Food Facts, da poiščemo izdelek. Ko zahtevaš oceno z AI – iz fotografije obroka, opisa ali recepta – se fotografija ali besedilo prek našega strežnika pošlje ponudniku Anthropic, ki vrne ocenjena živila in hranila. Fotografij na strežniku ne hranimo. Ocene hranil so približne in niso zdravniški nasvet.

Apple Health in Health Connect (neobvezno)

Če povežeš Apple Health (iPhone) ali Health Connect (Android), Forkful bere tvoje korake, vadbe s porabljenimi kalorijami in težo (npr. s pametne tehtnice). V tvoje zdravstvene podatke ne zapisuje ničesar. Koraki in vadbe ostanejo samo na tvoji napravi in služijo prikazu aktivnosti ter – če to izbereš – prištevanju kalorij vadbe dnevnemu cilju. Meritve teže se dodajo v dnevnik teže v aplikaciji, ki se sinhronizira z računom le, če ga uporabljaš. Zdravstvenih podatkov nikoli ne prodajamo, ne delimo s tretjimi, ne uporabljamo za oglaševanje in jih ne shranjujemo v iCloud. Povezavo lahko kadarkoli prekineš v aplikaciji ali odvzameš dostop v aplikaciji Zdravje oziroma v nastavitvah Health Connect.

Opomniki, postenje in pripomočki

Opomniki so na tvoji napravi načrtovani kot lokalna obvestila; storitve za potisna obvestila ne uporabljamo. Časovnik posta se shrani skupaj s tvojimi ostalimi podatki. Pripomočki na začetnem zaslonu prikazujejo današnje številke, ki jih aplikacija z njimi deli samo na tvoji napravi.

Uvoz receptov

Ko uvoziš recept s povezave, aplikacija (ali naš strežnik pri spletni različici) prenese to spletno stran, da prebere recept. Povezav, ki jih uvoziš, ne beležimo.

Strežnik in dnevniki

Naš strežnik gostuje pri ponudniku Render v Evropski uniji (Frankfurt). Kot vsak spletni strežnik za kratek čas obdela tvoj IP-naslov, da odgovori na zahteve in prepreči zlorabe (omejevanje števila zahtev). Ne uporabljamo orodij za analitiko, sledenje ali oglaševanje.

Tvoje pravice

Svoje podatke lahko v aplikaciji kadarkoli pregledaš, popraviš, izvoziš in izbrišeš. V EU imaš pravice po Splošni uredbi o varstvu podatkov (GDPR), vključno s pravico do pritožbe pri Informacijskem pooblaščencu.

Otroci

Forkful ni namenjen otrokom, mlajšim od 13 let, in zavestno ne zbira njihovih podatkov.
